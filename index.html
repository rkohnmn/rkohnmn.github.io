01 / Introduction

More than
one subject.

A student, teammate, builder, and future teacher. The connections are the interesting part.

I’m pursuing three majors at Muhlenberg College because I enjoy the questions each one asks.

AI: Enhancing capability with reasoning intact.

Generative AI is a tool that enhances human capability much like a calculator in a mathematics classroom: it accelerates routine computation and exploratory inquiry, but formulating the problem, critically assessing intermediate steps, and interpreting the conceptual outcome must remain transparent and student-owned.

In my classroom, technology will elevate rather than replace student cognition. The fundamental standard remains: Can the student explain their method, justify their assumptions, verify the mathematics, and defend their conclusions?
