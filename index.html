Advanced Physics Instructional Sample · Advanced Undergraduate Quantum Mechanics

Sequential Stern–Gerlach Measurements for Spin-1 Particles

Demonstrates instructional design and scaffolding for a three-state quantum system ($S=1$). Rather than treating quantum measurement as abstract linear algebra, the lesson contrasts spin-1 with spin-1/2 systems, leverages an intuitive geometric basis-change analogy, and confronts the pervasive student misconception that perpendicular filter measurements yield equal 50/50 probabilities.

Advanced Physics Instructional Project · Advanced Undergraduate Physics / Quantum Optics

Photon Polarization as a Spin-1 Quantum System

An integrated instructional project pairing a rigorous written lesson plan with an accompanying 11-slide pedagogical presentation. The project clarifies the critical physical distinction between massive spin-1 systems and massless relativistic photons (helicity $\lambda = \pm 1$), establishes circular polarization states as eigenstates of $J_z$, executes Townsend Example 2.8 basis transformations, and bridges matrix mechanics to optical waveplates and photon entanglement.

AI: Enhancing capability with reasoning intact.

Generative AI is a tool that enhances human capability much like a calculator in a mathematics classroom: it accelerates routine computation and exploratory inquiry, but formulating the problem, critically assessing intermediate steps, and interpreting the conceptual outcome must remain transparent and student-owned.

In my classroom, technology will elevate rather than replace student cognition. The fundamental standard remains: Can the student explain their method, justify their assumptions, verify the mathematics, and defend their conclusions?
